Q02 - label limitation, not a demonstrated factual retrieval miss
The benchmark named only LLD section 1 as its expected evidence. That anchor was
not selected, so the recorded score is zero. However, the rank-1 result is the
addressing.csv row for LON-VRRP, VLAN 120, 10.20.20.1/24. This directly supports
the question's main facts. Ranks 6 and 9 contain the London physical user gateways.
The original annotation therefore omits valid alternative evidence and produces
a false negative. Preserve the original result; do not characterize it as a bot
failure or silently revise the published score. A versioned benchmark revision
should add the specific addressing row and be evaluated again for both modes.

Q05 and Q11 - ranking weakness
Expected anchors appeared within the selected top 20 but outside the top 5.
Evidence availability and high-rank relevance are separate properties. This run
establishes the position of authored anchors, not whether every earlier result
was irrelevant. Inspect the saved trace before attributing a cause.

Correct routes do not prove relevance, semantic sufficiency or factual accuracy.

this authored development set. Ranking needs further evaluation, and annotation
